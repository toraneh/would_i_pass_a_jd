# Would I Pass a J.D.? Topic Selection in Constitutional Law Examinations: A Comparative Analysis of the J.D. and LL.B. Programs

A comparative analysis of constitutional law topic selection in US and Indian legal education, utilizing publicly available examination repositories from US National University and Indian PAH Solapur University.

## Overview

This study examines the alignment and divergence in constitutional law topics tested across J.D. (Juris Doctor, United States) and LL.B. (Bachelor of Laws, India) examinations. The analysis uses publicly available examination records to characterize curricula and assess whether topic emphasis differs between the two legal education systems.

## Data Sources

- **US National University:** Public examination repository (J.D. program)
- **PAH Solapur University:** Public examination repository (LL.B. program)
